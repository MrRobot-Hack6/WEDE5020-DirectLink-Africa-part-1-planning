## Project Proposal - Direct-Link Africa.

## Organisation Overview.

1.	Name: Direct-Link Africa (Non-Profit Organisation)

2.	Description of organisation: Direct-Link Africa was created to solve key issues in the donations of humanitarian aid. A non-profit logistics organization created to replace cash drives with item pledging, while being transparent giving from all around the world directly to Northern and Sub-Saharan Africa. 

3.	Mission: Link donors with regional shortages.

4.	Vision: Creating a world where donated goods match up with the needs of the community.

5.	Target I hope to impact: Direct-Link Africa wants to impact Gen-Z and Millennials that want to give back as well as community-based partners (church groups, corporate teams). 

## Website Goals and Objectives. 

•	Goals: The Goal would be to build a website to help those in need, coming across people who can help or want to help the less fortunate communities.

•	Key Performance Indicators: According to CauseVox (2026), for to consider a cause effective in terms of engagement, you need to have a system of visitor behavioural tracking that allows you to measure success by landing page success rates. The tracking software that should combine actual identification of donor profile along with the accurate measurement of the location and status of inventory, that the most non-profit warehouses serve only as distribution hubs, passing product across directly to the intended recipient programs (NetSuite, 2026).

## Proposed Website Features and Functionality

1.	Homepage: The home page will have a Hero image with a short motivation on why donations are needed, at the right corner of the homepage would be the about us, contact page, and services page.
2.	About us: The about us page will explain the mission we have with the donations that get sent to Direct-Link Africa, explaining in detail what they will be doing and the impact each donation will have.
3.	Contact page: The contact page will have an email address, an Address, and a cellphone number.
4.  Enquiries page: The Enquiries page will have an personal details form, where all personal details needs be filled in for making donations to the organization.
5.	Services: The services page will be where the donation forms are being filled in and where the donations will be made
