DirectLink Africa is a non-profit Organization. We don't use physical currency, but instead rely on donations and support from individuals and organizations to fund our programs and initiatives. Our team is made up of dedicated volunteers who are passionate about making a difference in the lives of those we serve.


Companies and individuals can support our mission by donating goods, volunteering their time and expertise, or partnering with us on specific projects. We welcome all forms of support and are committed to transparency and accountability in all our operations.


We believe that every person deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential, regardless of their background or circumstances.
